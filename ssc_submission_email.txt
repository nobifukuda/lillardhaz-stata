Package: lillardhaz
Author: Nobutaka Fukuda, Tohoku University <nobutaka.fukuda@tohoku.ac.jp>
Requires: Stata 17 or later

Description:

lillardhaz fits two-equation simultaneous models in the style of Lillard
(1993, Journal of Econometrics): a pair of processes -- each a binary
probit outcome or a continuous-time hazard duration -- linked through a
single correlation parameter between their underlying error terms,
estimated jointly by maximum likelihood via a Gaussian copula. Equation 1
may be a probit, a log-normal accelerated-failure-time hazard, or a
piecewise (linear-log-hazard) Gompertz hazard with an arbitrary,
user-specified number of segments; equation 2 is always a hazard,
log-normal or piecewise Gompertz. A nocorr option fits the two equations
independently (correlation fixed at 0), which is shown in the
documentation to reduce exactly to two separate univariate fits -- a
natural nested baseline for a likelihood-ratio test of correlation.

This is a from-scratch implementation built to replace a family of
widely-circulated ml programs (for the various probit/hazard combinations,
with and without correlation) that used an algebraically incorrect
bivariate likelihood in the correlated case. Every eq1() x eq2()
combination, correlated and independent, was verified by simulating
20,000-25,000 observations under known parameters and confirming the
command recovers them; the verification scripts and their output logs are
included in the package repository. See the enclosed lillardhaz.sthlp
(and the fuller derivation at
https://github.com/nobifukuda/lillardhaz-stata/blob/main/docs/manual.html)
for the model, the joint likelihood for every equation-type combination,
and the verification results.

The package has been tested with net install from a local directory and
installs and runs cleanly.

Files enclosed (also at https://github.com/nobifukuda/lillardhaz-stata):

  lillardhaz_d0.ado        ml d0 evaluator
